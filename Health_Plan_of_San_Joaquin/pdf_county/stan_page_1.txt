Introduction
This Provider and Pharmacy Directory includes information about the provider and pharmacy
types in Health Plan of San Joaquin/Mountain Valley Health Plan Advantage D-SNP and listings
of all the plan’s providers and pharmacies as of the date of this Directory. The listings contain
address and contact information as well as other details such as days and hours of operations,
specialties, and skills. Key terms and their definitions appear in alphabetical order in the last
chapter of the Member Handbook.
